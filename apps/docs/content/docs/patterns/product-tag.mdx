From Figma 10 Instagram Storefront, Product Tag (`7275:19846`, with the orphan master `772:4390`
the screens use) on the mobile post view's media (`7275:20923`). Uses
[Popover](/docs/components/popover/), [Button](/docs/components/button/),
[NumberValue](/docs/components/number-value/), [Text](/docs/components/text/) and the `Tag` and
`ArrowRight` glyphs of [`@nizivo/icons`](/docs/icons/).

## Notes

- **A real button, named.** Each dot is the Popover's own trigger, a `<button>` named "Product:
  Moss mug" that says whether its popover is open. It is 24 px, and the trigger's hit area grows to
  44 px on a coarse pointer, without changing the dot (Figma's dot is 24 px, under `size/touch-min`,
  §3.3). The photo is a `<figure>` named "Products tagged in this photo", its
  caption the hint, and the dots are in reading order (by y,
  then x), so Tab moves through them as the eye does.
- **Open, close, focus.** Opening moves focus to the product link; Escape or a press outside closes
  it and focus returns to the dot. Only the popover is client code: the photo stays
  server-rendered. Pair the photo with the [Tagged product](/docs/patterns/tagged-product/) list,
  which gives the same products without the photo.
- **Where it differs from Figma.** The popover is the kit's Popover surface with a title, the price
  in tabular figures and a link, not Figma's dark chip: the chip's price is `text/tertiary` on
  `bg/inverse`, 4.03:1, under AA (§3.3). The Popover moves to another side when the end side has no
  room, where Figma always opens to the right. The dot and the hint use the on-media pair of the
  [Shoppable post](/docs/patterns/shoppable-post/) (`--nz-bg-code` with `--nz-text-on-dark`), so
  they read on any photo in both themes. There is no pulse: an endless animation needs a reduced
  motion story the kit does not have yet. Positions are fractions of the photo (0 to 1), so a tag
  stays on its product at any size.
