## Notes

- **Client JavaScript.** ChipGroup and its Field are client components. The chips are native radios
  in a `<form>`, so the choice works and submits before they hydrate.
- **Native radios.** Each group is a radio group named by its Field's label ("Colour", "Size"):
  Tab enters the group, arrow keys move the choice, and the form submits `color` and `size`. No
  headless library is needed.
- **Unavailable, not disabled.** Figma's Disabled state greys the chip and replaces the colour.
  Here an out-of-stock option keeps its colour, is struck through and is described as "Sold out",
  and stays focusable, so a screen reader user learns it exists and an app can offer "Notify me".
  Add `disabled` only for an option that cannot be chosen at all.
- **Colour is data.** Figma binds the swatch to a chart token; the swatch is the variant's own
  colour, passed as the leading element's background, with a thin ring so a light colour such as
  Sand shows on white.
- **No layout shift.** Figma's selected chip grows from 90 × 40 to 92 × 42 as its stroke thickens;
  the chips keep their size when chosen.
- **Spelling.** The visible label is "Colour", as Figma writes it; the form field is `color`.
