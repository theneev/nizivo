---
title: Shoppable post
description: A post's media as one link, with a Shop badge that counts its tagged products and reads in both themes, alone and in the storefront's grid. A server composition; it ships no JavaScript.
---

It uses [Badge](/docs/components/badge/) and the `ShoppingBag` and `Layers`
glyphs of [`@nizivo/icons`](/docs/icons/).

## Notes

- **Text on media.** The badge and the carousel glyph sit on `--nz-bg-code` with
  `--nz-text-on-dark`: both stay dark and white in light and dark mode, and the tokens' contrast
  check holds the pair at 17.4:1 in light and 18.7:1 in dark. The surface is opaque, so no photo
  under it can lower that. The design's `#141714` at 62 % with `text/inverse` falls to 1.12:1 in dark
  mode, because `text/inverse` turns dark there. The kit has no on-media token yet;
  this pair stands in for one.
- **One link per tile.** The link's name says what the tile shows: "Post from 12 October, 3
  products to shop", and ", 4 photos" for a carousel. The badge and the glyph are hidden from
  screen readers, so nothing is read twice and nothing in the tile is a second control.
- **Where it differs from the design.** No hover-only "Shop 3 items" call to action: the design shows it
  only on hover and hides the count while it does, which leaves touch and keyboard users without
  it. The badge is always there and the tile has a focus ring. The corner radius is `radius/xs`
  (4 px) for the design's off-scale 6 px. The carousel glyph gets the same dark backing as the badge,
  so it shows on a light photo.
