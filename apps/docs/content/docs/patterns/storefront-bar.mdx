From Figma 10 Instagram Storefront, Storefront Bar (`7275:19889`, with the orphan master `772:4457`
the screens use) and the profile header of the mobile storefront (`7275:20706`). Uses
[Avatar](/docs/components/avatar/), [Badge](/docs/components/badge/),
[Button](/docs/components/button/), [IconButton](/docs/components/icon-button/),
[NumberValue](/docs/components/number-value/), [Text](/docs/components/text/), the `ShoppingBag`
glyph of [`@nizivo/icons`](/docs/icons/) and the `Instagram` brand glyph of
`@nizivo/icons/brands/instagram`.

## Notes

- **The bag says its count.** The IconButton is named "Bag, 2 items" and links to the bag; the
  count badge on it is hidden from screen readers and takes no press. Past 99 it shows "99+", and
  with an empty bag it shows nothing. The button is 40 px and its hit area grows to 44 px on a
  coarse pointer (Figma's bag is 40 px, §3.5).
- **The Instagram glyph.** Its usage rule allows it only on links that go to Instagram, so here it
  is a link to the store's profile, named "Moss & Kiln on Instagram", with a 24 px target (44 px on
  a coarse pointer). Do not use it as decoration.
- **Followers.** `NumberValue` with `notation: 'compact'` prints 12,480 as "12.5K" in English and
  as the locale writes it elsewhere.
- **Where it differs from Figma.** No search: put a [SearchField](/docs/components/search-field/)
  between the identity and the actions when the store has one (Figma's mobile search is a bare
  20 px icon with no button, §3.5). The Follow Button and the follower count come from the
  storefront's profile header (§4), where Figma draws them as custom frames. There is no
  `Platform` variant: when the bar is too narrow for one row, the actions wrap under the store's
  name, and a long name is cut with an ellipsis.
