## Notes

- **The Avatar** is decorative, because the name is printed beside it, and shows the initials when
  there is no photo, which Figma's raw ellipse cannot. Pass `src` only for a photo that is known to
  load.
- **How to reach them.** Figma's doc text promises it and draws no action; here the email is a
  `mailto:` link. Add a call or message action beside it if your app has one.
- **The figures** are a description list: each label is a `<dt>`, each figure a `<dd>`, so they are
  read as pairs. Money is `NumberValue` with the store's currency, and the counts go through
  `Intl.NumberFormat`.
- **Contrast.** Figma's stat labels are `text/tertiary` on `bg/subtle`, 4.16:1; here they are
  `text/secondary`.
- **Tags** are a list of Badges without a dot: the words are the tags, and no colour means more
  than its word.
