From Figma 10 Instagram Storefront, Tagged Product (`7275:19862`, with the orphan master `772:4414`
the screens use), as the "In this post" list of the mobile post view (`7275:20903`). Uses
[IconButton](/docs/components/icon-button/), [NumberValue](/docs/components/number-value/),
[Text](/docs/components/text/) and the `Plus` and `Check` glyphs of
[`@nizivo/icons`](/docs/icons/).

## Notes

- **Named action, 44 px on touch.** The IconButton's name is "Add Moss mug to bag", and its
  Tooltip shows the same words. It is 40 px, Figma's size, and its hit area grows to 44 px on a
  coarse pointer (Figma's 40 px button is under `size/touch-min`, §3.4).
- **Added, in words too.** The button is a toggle: `aria-pressed` says whether the product is in the
  bag, the check replaces the plus, and the row says "In your bag" next to the variant. Figma shows
  the added state only by the icon and the fill (§3.4). When the shopper presses it, announce the
  change ("Added Moss mug to bag") with the app's live region or a Toast.
- **Where it differs from Figma.** The variant line is `text/secondary`: Figma's `text/tertiary`
  is 4.03 to 4.31:1, under AA for 14 px text (§3.4). The product's name is the link to its page; the
  row is not one big link, so the button is not nested in it.
