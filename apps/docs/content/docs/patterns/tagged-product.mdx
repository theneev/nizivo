## Notes

- **Named action, 44 px on touch.** The IconButton's name is "Add Moss mug to bag", and its
  Tooltip shows the same words. It is 40 px, the design's size, and its hit area grows to 44 px on a
  coarse pointer (the design's 40 px button is under `size/touch-min`).
- **Added, in words too.** The button is a toggle: `aria-pressed` says whether the product is in the
  bag, the check replaces the plus, and the row says "In your bag" next to the variant. The design shows
  the added state only by the icon and the fill. When the shopper presses it, announce the
  change ("Added Moss mug to bag") with the app's live region or a Toast.
- **Where it differs from the design.** The variant line is `text/secondary`: the design's
  `text/tertiary` is 4.03 to 4.31:1, under AA for 14 px text. The product's name is the link to its
  page; the row is not one big link, so the button is not nested in it.
