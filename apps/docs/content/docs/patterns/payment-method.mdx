## Notes

- **Client JavaScript.** ChoiceCard and its Field are client components. The radios are native and
  in a `<form>`, so the choice works and submits before they hydrate.
- **A radio group.** The design draws a fake radio (an ellipse with a 5 px stroke, 1.57:1 against
  white). Here each card is a label around a native radio, the group is named "Payment method" by
  its Field, and arrow keys move the choice.
- **Spoken in words.** A card's name is "Visa ending in 4242, Default": the bullets are hidden and
  "ending in" is visually hidden. Its description is "Expires August 2028" for screen readers, built
  with `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, while the screen shows the design's "Expires 08/28".
- **Brand marks.** The generic card glyph stands in for the design's 44 × 30 mark. Card brands' logos
  have their own rules and colours, so they are the app's to supply; the brand's name is in the
  text either way.
- **No layout shift.** The design's selected row grows from 73 to 74 px as its stroke thickens;
  ChoiceCard keeps its size when chosen.
- **Adding a card** is a link drawn as a tertiary Button, below the group, which the design does not
  draw.
