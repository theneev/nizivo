title: Shipment tracker
description: A shipment's steps as an ordered list, the current one marked with aria-current="step" and every step's status said in words, a column on a narrow card and a row on a wide one. A server composition; it ships no JavaScript.
---

It uses [Card](/docs/components/card/) and
[Text](/docs/components/text/), with the `Receipt`, `Package`, `Truck` and `House` icons from
[`@nizivo/icons`](/docs/icons/). There is no Steps component, so the steps live in this pattern's
stylesheet.

## Notes

- **An ordered list.** The steps are an `<ol>` named "Progress"; the current step carries
  `aria-current="step"`, and every step's name is followed by its status in visually hidden words
  ("Completed", "Current step", "Not started"). The icons and the lines between the nodes are
  decorative.
- **Not colour alone.** The current step is bold on a solid node, a completed step's node is
  tinted and its line is green, and an upcoming step has a dashed ring: the design's upcoming node,
  `bg/subtle`, is 1.04:1 on white and all but invisible.
- **Four steps.** The design's notes name four steps but its Step axis has three (no Ordered); here
  Ordered is the first step. Completed steps carry their date and time, as the design draws them; an
  upcoming step carries none, where the design prints a dash a screen reader would read.
- **Narrow and wide.** The design's four 150 px columns do not fit a phone and overflow in longer
  languages. The tracker is a column until its card is 34rem wide (a container query, so it follows
  the space it is given, not the viewport), then a row.
- **RTL.** The lines are placed with logical properties, so the row and its lines run right to
  left. The truck is not mirrored: it is not a directional icon.
- **Dates.** `Intl.DateTimeFormat` with an explicit locale and time zone, inside `<time
dateTime>`; an app passes the customer's time zone.
