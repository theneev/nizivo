## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Where it goes

Put it next to the content it describes, never as decoration: under a generated product
description, beside an edited caption, in an answer's header. `variant="subtle"` (the default) is
for text on a page; `variant="solid"` stands out over busier surfaces. Lime (`bg/accent`) marks AI
in Nizivo and nothing else.

## Words

It says "AI generated". Pass children, or `labels={{ label: 'Edited with AI' }}`, to say something
more exact. The sparkles mark is decorative; the words carry the meaning.
