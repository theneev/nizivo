## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript of its own: each chip is a button, and what its `onClick` does is your
component's code. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Fill, don't send

A chip puts its words in the composer; people still press send. Do that in `onClick`, and move focus
to the composer so they can edit. Keep about five in view at most, under an empty composer or after
an answer as follow-ups.

## The group

`SuggestionGroup` wraps the chips with 8 px between them and is a group named "Suggestions"
(`labels.label`, or your own `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`).
