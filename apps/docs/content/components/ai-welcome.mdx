## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript of its own; its children (a
[PromptComposer](/docs/components/prompt-composer/)) may have some.
[How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Content

`greeting` is the heading (`headingLevel`, 2 by default); `description` says what the assistant can
see and do. Be honest about its scope and its access to data. The children follow at full width:
a [SuggestionGroup](/docs/components/suggestion-chip/) of starters and the composer.

## The orb

The orb is decoration, drawn with CSS as [VoiceOrb](/docs/components/voice-orb/)'s is; `orb` takes
any picture in its place, hidden from assistive technology.
