---
title: Switch
description: An on or off setting that applies at once, drawn as a track and thumb. A native checkbox with role switch, so it submits with the form before hydration.
apg: https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/switch/
keyboard:
  - keys: Tab
    action: Moves focus to the switch.
  - keys: Space
    action: Turns it on or off.
keyboardSource: a native `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">` (`switch.client.tsx`), which adds no key handling.
