## Static or interactive

**Server.** `PageHeader` renders a `<header>` with no client JavaScript. What you put in `actions`
keeps its own tier: a Button stays server-rendered, a SegmentedControl hydrates.
[How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

In a client app (a Vite or React Router single-page app, or a `'use client'` file) it is an ordinary React component: event handlers, refs and state work as usual. [In a client app](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/#in-a-client-app).

## One heading per page

`title` renders the page's `<h1>`. The shell's top bar shows the section name as text, not as a
second heading. Inside a page whose `<h1>` is elsewhere, pass `level={2}`; the title keeps its look.

## Layout

The title (Heading/H1) and the controls sit on one row, 16 px apart and centred, the
controls at the row's end. `meta` sits beside the title, for example a status `<Badge>`;
`description` is a Body/M line under it. Below the `sm` breakpoint (40rem) the title takes
Heading/H2's size, as 08's mobile frame does, and the controls move to a row of their own. Hooks: `--nz-page-header-gap`, `--nz-page-header-title-font`.
