title: Surface
description: A panel of the page's surface colour, solid or glass. Glass is the surface at 72 % over a blurred backdrop, for a panel over a photo, a shader or a busy page, and it stays opaque wherever the blur is not available.
---

## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript: it is a styled element.
[How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Solid or glass

`material="solid"` (the default) paints `bg/surface` with a hairline border and `radius/xl`.
`material="glass"` is Figma's Surface/Glass style: `bg/surface` at 72 % with a 24 px blur of
whatever is behind it, and a soft shadow, as on the floating navbar over a shader hero. Put it over
an image or colour, as the design says ("always on a tinted card, never on white"); over a plain
white page it looks like a solid panel.

`render` makes it any element: `render={<nav aria-label="Main" />}` for a navbar,
`render={<section />}` for a card.

## Where glass stays opaque

The text on glass keeps the surface's contrast because the panel falls back to solid whenever the
blur cannot happen or should not:

- in browsers without `backdrop-filter` (Safari before 18, the floor's Safari 17.5);
- when the reader asks for reduced transparency (`prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`);
- in forced colours, where it is the system's `Canvas`.

## Styling

Surface has no public hooks (`--nz-surface-*` names design tokens), so set its padding, radius or
border with your own class or `style`.
