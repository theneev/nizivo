## The trigger

Always compose a Button into the trigger:

```tsx
<Tooltip.Trigger render={<Button variant="tertiary">Export</Button>} />
```

For an icon-only action, use an [IconButton](/docs/components/icon-button/): it pairs itself with a
Tooltip that shows its `label`, so it needs no `Tooltip.Root`.

A bare `<Tooltip.Trigger>` is a plain `<button>` with no size of its own. Around a short text or a
single glyph it is smaller than the 24 by 24 CSS pixel minimum target of WCAG 2.2 success criterion
2.5.8 (Target Size, Minimum). One on its own can pass through the criterion's spacing exception, but
two side by side fail it. Whether a bare trigger should get a minimum size of its own is open.
