---
title: Tooltip
description: A short label shown on hover and keyboard focus, on an inverse surface. The trigger can be any focusable element, a server Button included.
apg: https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tooltip/
keyboard:
  - keys: Tab
    action: Focusing the trigger with the keyboard shows the tooltip at once; the trigger points at it with aria-describedby.
  - keys: Escape
    action: Hides the tooltip; focus stays on the trigger.
keyboardSource: react-aria-components 1.22.1 `TooltipTrigger` (react-aria 3.53.1 `useTooltipTrigger`) through `_engines/react-aria/tooltip.client.tsx`. Showing on keyboard focus and hiding on Escape are checked by the Next fixture and by apps/docs/scripts/smoke.ts.
---
