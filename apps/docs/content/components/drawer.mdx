## Static or interactive

**Client.** It is the Dialog's engine at an edge: `@nizivo/react/drawer` measures 31.7 KiB gzip with
React Aria inside, almost all of it shared with Dialog (size-limit, all locales;
`packages/react/SIZES.md`). [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Sides

`Drawer.Popup` takes `side`:

- `end` (the default): the right edge in a left-to-right page, the left edge in a right-to-left one.
  This is Figma's Drawer: 440 px wide, the full height, rounded on its two inner corners.
- `start`: the opposite edge.
- `bottom`: a bottom sheet, the full width and as tall as its content, rounded on its top corners.
  It is the "More" sheet AppShell will open below 640 px.

`data-side` on the popup (and its positioner) is the physical edge it sits at: `right`, `left` or
`bottom`. A narrow viewport keeps a 3rem strip of the scrim beside (or above) the drawer, so there is
always somewhere to press to close it. Set `--nz-drawer-width` for another width, and
`--nz-drawer-radius` and `--nz-drawer-bg` to restyle the panel.

## Motion

The drawer slides its whole width (or height) in from its edge on `duration/slow` with
`easing/enter`, and out on `duration/base` with `easing/exit`; the scrim only fades. Under reduced
motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` or `[data-nz-motion="reduce"]`) it does not slide: it only
fades, in 120 ms.

## Parts

`Drawer.Header` holds the title and, under it, a description or badges, above a rule.
`Drawer.Body` is the only scroller, and fills the height between the header and the footer.
`Drawer.Footer` holds the actions, which share its width, above a rule.

`<Drawer.Close />` with no `render` and no `children` is an IconButton (tertiary, `md`) showing the
close glyph, named "Close". Placed as a direct child of `Drawer.Popup`, it sits in the header's end
corner, where Figma draws the close glyph, and the header leaves room for it. With `children` it is a
plain button; with `render`, your element, for example `render={<Button>Cancel</Button>}`.

## Focus return

As the Dialog's: `returnFocus` on `Drawer.Root` (an element ref or a function that returns one, while
it is on the page), else the trigger, else the trigger of the overlay the drawer was opened from,
else `<body>` with a development warning. `returnFocus={false}` leaves focus alone.

## Inside other overlays

A Drawer opened from a Dialog, or a Dialog opened from a Drawer, adds no second scrim; the one
underneath is marked `data-nested-open`. A Popover, Select, Menu or Tooltip opened inside a Drawer
portals into it and paints above it. Escape and a press outside close only the topmost overlay, and
the page stays scroll-locked until the last one closes.
