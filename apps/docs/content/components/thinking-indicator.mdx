## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript: the dots are CSS. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Name the step

It says "Thinking…". When the assistant knows what it is doing, say it: children replace the label
(`<ThinkingIndicator>Reading last week's orders…</ThinkingIndicator>`). Replace the indicator with the
answer once the first words arrive.

## Screen readers and motion

The indicator is `role="status"`, so a screen reader announces the label, and each new step, politely.
The dots are decorative. Under reduced motion they stand still and only the label changes.
