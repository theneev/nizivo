---
title: Select
description: A single-choice list box with a button trigger, groups and separators, backed by a hidden native select so forms submit and reset as usual.
apg: https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/listbox/
keyboard:
  - keys: Enter, Space, ArrowDown
    action: On the trigger, opens the list and focuses the selected option, else the first.
  - keys: ArrowUp
    action: On the trigger, opens the list and focuses the selected option, else the last.
  - keys: ArrowLeft, ArrowRight
    action: On the closed trigger, selects the previous or next option without opening the list.
  - keys: Type a character
    action: Selects (on the trigger) or focuses (in the list) the next option whose label starts with what you typed.
  - keys: ArrowDown, ArrowUp
    action: In the open list, moves focus to the next or previous option, skipping disabled ones.
  - keys: Home, End, PageUp, PageDown
    action: In the open list, moves focus to the first or last option, or a page at a time.
  - keys: Enter, Space
    action: In the open list, selects the focused option and closes the list.
  - keys: Escape
    action: Closes the list without changing the selection and returns focus to the trigger.
keyboardSource: react-aria-components 1.22.1 `Select` (react-aria 3.53.1 `useSelect`, `useMenuTrigger` and `useSelectableCollection`) through `_engines/react-aria/select.client.tsx`. apps/docs/scripts/smoke.ts checks ArrowDown opening on the selected option, ArrowDown and Enter choosing, Escape closing with focus back on the trigger, ArrowRight and ArrowLeft on the closed trigger, and typing on the trigger.
