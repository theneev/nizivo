## Static or interactive

**Client.** It is controls only. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Wiring it

Pass `playing`, `currentTime` and `duration` (in seconds) from your `<audio>` or `<video>`, and
handle `onPlayingChange`, `onSeek`, `onSkipBack` and `onSkipForward`. It never starts playback on
its own, so it never autoplays.

## Accessibility

Play and Pause are one button whose name changes with its icon. The scrubber is a
[Slider](/docs/components/slider/) named "Seek" and read as "1 minute 12 seconds of 3 minutes 48
seconds"; its arrow keys seek 5 seconds. Figma also asks for `aria-pressed` on the play button,
but a name that changes already says the state, and the two together say it twice.
