## Static or interactive

**Client.** The server renders a poster in the same colours, drawn with CSS; once the page
hydrates, a canvas draws the shader over it from its first frame. A browser without WebGL keeps the
poster. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Variants

`variant` is the design's Shader Surface type, and each follows the theme:

- **Aurora** (`aurora`, the default): soft curtains of light that sway, lime over a pale ground with
  mint below.
- **Contour** (`contour`): the contour lines of a slowly drifting landscape, pale, with one level in
  six drawn bold.
- **Dot Matrix** (`dot-matrix`): a grid of dots in slow waves, lime where it is brightest. Always
  dark, as the design draws it.
- **Glow Orb** (`glow-orb`): [GlowOrb](/docs/components/glow-orb/)'s sphere in a glow that spreads
  over the box.
- **Rays** (`rays`): fine strands of green, lime and warm light, some soft, leaning and waving.
- **Mesh** (`mesh`): a soft field of mint, lime, green and the page's ground, drifting.

`aurora-dark`, `contour-dark` and `glow-orb-dark` keep their dark colours on a light page, for a band
that is always dark. The design's Rays use an amber that is not in the palette; here the warm
strands take `icon/warning`. Each variant's shader loads when the first surface of that variant
mounts, so a page downloads only the ones it shows.

## In a component

It fills its box. Pass it to the components the design draws with it:
[CtaBand](/docs/components/cta-band/)'s `background` (`<ShaderSurface variant="aurora-dark" />`),
[BentoTile](/docs/components/bento-tile/)'s `background` (Aurora for a wide tile, Glow Orb for a
square one, Contour for a tall one, as the design draws them) and
[ProfileCard](/docs/components/profile-card/)'s `cover`. Each drops its CSS background behind it, and
CtaBand keeps a soft dark scrim behind its text, so the text stays readable whatever drifts behind.

## Light on a phone

No library: one fragment shader on WebGL 1, on the same small runtime as
[GlowOrb](/docs/components/glow-orb/). It draws only while it is on screen and the page is visible,
at a pixel ratio of at most 2, and gives its WebGL context back when it unmounts (a page holds about
sixteen at once). Under Reduced motion it draws one still frame. In forced colours the
canvas is hidden and the system's colours show.

## Colours

Each variant reads four of Nizivo's colours, chosen to match the design's. Set
`--nz-shader-surface-ground` and `--nz-shader-surface-tone-1` to `-tone-3` to recolour any variant;
the stylesheet says which tone is which in each. It is decoration, hidden from assistive technology; never set text on it without a surface of
its own.
