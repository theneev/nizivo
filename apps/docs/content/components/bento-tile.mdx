## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

`title` (h3 by default) and `description` sit on the frosted caption card, never on the
background. `size` spans the tile in a 12-column grid: `wide` 8 columns, `square` 4, `tall` 4 by 2
rows. `background` takes a screenshot or a photo; without one, CSS gradients stand in for the
design's shader, which waits for a later package. Without backdrop blur, or with reduced
transparency, the card is opaque.
