title: TopBar
description: The bar above the page on screens 640 px and wider, with the page's name or a Breadcrumb at the start, the search, then notifications and the account.
---

## Static or interactive

**Server.** It is a layout with slots and ships no client JavaScript of its own. What you put in the
slots brings its own: the examples' SearchField and IconButton are client components. [How to
choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

In a client app (a Vite or React Router single-page app, or a `'use client'` file) it is an ordinary React component: event handlers, refs and state work as usual. [In a client app](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/#in-a-client-app).

## Slots

- `title`: the page's name, in Heading/H3's look. It is text, not a heading: the page's `PageHeader`
  holds its one `<h1>`.
- `start`: in place of `title`, for example a `Breadcrumb` on a page deeper in the app.
- `search`: usually a `SearchField` in a `<form method="get">`, a real input (M49). It is 360 px at most
  and gives up width first.
- `actions`: at the end, a notifications `IconButton` and the account's `Avatar` or menu.

## In the shell

`AppShell` shows the TopBar from 640 px and the `MobileTopBar` below that. The bar is not sticky: it
scrolls with the page. Its padding follows the grid's margin, so its contents line up with the page's.

Hooks: `--nz-top-bar-gap`, `--nz-top-bar-padding-inline`, `--nz-top-bar-search-width`,
`--nz-top-bar-bg`, `--nz-top-bar-border`.
