## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript: it is a link. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Linking

`number` is the source's number as the answer counts them, and `href` where it is: `#source-1` for a
[SourceCard](/docs/components/source-card/) under the answer (give the card that `id`), or the
source's own URL. `title` adds the source's title to the accessible name: "Source 1: Moss & Kiln
sales report". `labels.source` and `labels.sourceTitled` change those words.

## Not yet

The design also shows the source in a popover when the citation is hovered or focused. That is not built
yet; the link goes to the source.
