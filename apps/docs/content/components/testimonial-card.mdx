## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

`quote` is a `<blockquote>` (`cite` takes its source) and `name`, `jobTitle` and `avatar` are its
`<figcaption>`. The quote mark is decoration. Use real, attributed quotes only.
