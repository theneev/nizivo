---
title: Button
description: A native button in five variants and three sizes, with a pending state that keeps focus. Compose it into triggers and links through render.
keyboard:
  - keys: Tab
    action: Moves focus to the button. A pending button stays focusable (aria-disabled); a disabled one does not.
  - keys: Enter, Space
    action: Activates the button. A pending button swallows the activation.
keyboardSource: a native `<button>` (`button.tsx`); with `render={<a />}` it is a link and only Enter activates it.
---
