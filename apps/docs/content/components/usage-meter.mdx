## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript: it is a native `<meter>` ([Meter](/docs/components/meter/)).
[How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Values

`value` is how much is used and `max` the allowance; the card shows "640 / 1,000" and a screen reader
hears "640 of 1,000 credits used" (`labels.valueText`, with `{value}` and `{max}`). `label` names it
("AI credits" by default) and `note` adds a line under the bar.

## Warning

From `warnAt` (0.8, so 80 %) the bar turns to `chart/warning`: Meter's high range, with `optimum` at
zero, since less used is better. Say what happens next in the note, and never stop people silently
when the allowance runs out.
