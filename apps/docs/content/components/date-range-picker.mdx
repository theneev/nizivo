## Unstable

It ships at `@nizivo/react/unstable/date-range-picker`, outside the semver promise, because the
design has not drawn an unavailable day or a comparison with the previous period yet. Its props may
change in a minor release.

## Static or interactive

**Client.** It is React Aria's RangeCalendar inside the Popover. The server renders the trigger,
with the applied range as its text, and the hidden form input; the popover, the calendar and
anything that depends on today's date render only after hydration, when it opens.
[How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Value

`value` and `defaultValue` are `{ start, end }`, ISO plain dates (`'2026-10-01'`), both ends
included, or `null`. Nothing is applied until Apply: choosing a preset or two days only changes what
the popover shows, and Cancel, Escape or a press outside keep the old range. `onValueChange` then
gets the range and `{ reason: 'preset' }` or `{ reason: 'calendar' }`. With `name`, the form gets one
field whose value is the ISO interval `2026-10-01/2026-10-07`.

`timeZone` is required: the presets and the today mark count from today in it. `min` and `max` limit
the days that can be picked.

## Presets

`presets` lists them in order: `today`, `yesterday`, `last-7-days`, `last-30-days`, `this-quarter`,
`year-to-date` (all six by default; `[]` for none). Each ends today. A chosen preset is shown pressed
until a day is picked.

## Reading the range

The trigger and the footer read the range as `labels.range`, `'{start} – {end}'` by default, each
end formatted on its own in `locale` ("Oct 1 – Oct 7, 2026"; the start drops the year when both
share it), never with `Intl.DateTimeFormat.formatRange`, whose spacing differs between browsers. Inside
a Field, the trigger is named by its value and the label, as Select's is, and a press on the label
focuses it without opening the popover.
