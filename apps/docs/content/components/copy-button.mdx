## Static or interactive

**Client.** It writes to the clipboard. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

`value` is the text to copy. After a copy the button reads "Copied" for 2 seconds, and a polite live
region says so; its name stays "Copy". `onCopied` hears the copy, and `onCopyFailed` hears why
the clipboard refused (nothing claims a copy then). It is drawn for the dark
[CodeBlock](/docs/components/code-block/) surface.
