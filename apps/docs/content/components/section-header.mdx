## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

`title` is the section's heading (`headingLevel`, h2 by default) and `description` one supporting
line. It is at most 720 px wide, so a line stays under about 75 characters. `align="center"` suits a
short, single-idea section. No eyebrow labels: the heading says it.
