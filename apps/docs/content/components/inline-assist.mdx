## Static or interactive

**Client.** A `role="toolbar"` that moves focus with the arrow keys, Home and End (mirrored right
to left). [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Actions

`actions` lists the actions, each with a `value`, a `label` and an `icon`; `onAction` gets the
value. "Ask AI…" comes last, after a divider, and calls `onAsk`.

## Where it appears

Placing it at the selection and opening it on Ctrl/⌘+J are the app's: the toolbar never takes
focus on its own. A result replaces the selection only after the person accepts it, with Undo.
