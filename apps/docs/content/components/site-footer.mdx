## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

It is a `<footer>`. `brand` is the logo (a link home) and `description` a line under it. Each of
`columns` (a `title` and its `links`) is a `<nav>` named by its heading. `legal` holds the name,
the version and the Privacy and Terms links. When narrow, the brand and
the columns wrap and stack, following their container, not the window.
