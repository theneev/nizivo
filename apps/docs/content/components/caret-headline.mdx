## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

The children are the heading (`level`, h1 by default). The caret blinks on and off every 530 ms; it
is decoration, hidden from assistive technology, and stays on under Reduced motion.
