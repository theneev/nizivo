## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

`title` is the card's heading (h3 by default), the children say the benefit and `icon` fills the
decorative tile. `action` takes a [Link](/docs/components/link/) whose words make sense on their
own ("Learn about accessibility", not "Learn more"). `variant` is `plain` on surfaces, `tinted` to
group and `inverse` for emphasis.
