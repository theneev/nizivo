## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## The date

`date` is a Date or an ISO 8601 string. The block shows the weekday and the day; it is
presentational, and the full date is a `<time datetime>` read with the details. `locale` formats it
and `timeZone` sets the zone: pass one, so the server's date and the browser's agree.

## Content

`title` is the card's heading (`headingLevel`, 3 by default), `when` the time and place, and
`attendees` who is going, best an [AvatarGroup](/docs/components/avatar/), followed by "going".
