## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript: the whole card is one link.
[How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Content

`title` names the area and the link, `description` says what is there, `meta` gives a real count
("114 components") and `icon` fills the tile. Keep counts true: generate them, never type them.
