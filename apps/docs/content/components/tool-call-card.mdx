## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript; a retry is a [Button](/docs/components/button/) you pass in
`actions`. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Status

`status` is the whole action's: `running` (a spinner, and the card is `aria-busy`), `done` or
`failed` (a critical edge). The pill always carries a word as well as its mark, so the state never
rests on colour; under Reduced motion the spinner stands still.

## Steps

`steps` is the plan, in order: each has a `label` and a `status` (`todo`, `running`, `done`,
`failed`). A screen reader hears each step's status after its label ("Apply tags, failed"). For a
failure, say which step and why in its label, and offer a retry of that step, not of the whole
action. Never hide an action the AI took.
