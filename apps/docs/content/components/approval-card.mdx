## Static or interactive

**Client.** It moves focus to Cancel when it appears and cancels on Escape.
[How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Content

`title` is the action ("Send 3 emails to customers?"), the children say what will happen and what
cannot be taken back, and `summary` sets out the details (the recipients, the subject, the amount).
`confirmLabel` names the action ("Send emails"), never "OK". `onEdit` adds an "Edit first" button.
`pending` shows the confirm in flight.

## Focus

As the design asks, focus lands on Cancel when the card mounts, without scrolling (`autoFocus={false}`
turns that off, for a card rendered with the page rather than in answer to the reader), and Escape
anywhere in the card calls `onCancel`. It is a section named by its title, not a modal: the
conversation stays readable around it. `headingLevel` (3 by default) fits it into the page outline.
