## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Reading it

The stars are decoration: the whole is one image named in words, "Rated 4.8 out of 5 from 1,208
reviews". `value` is the score, `max` the scale (5), `count` the number of reviews, and `locale`
formats the numbers. A star fills for each whole point. It is not an input: to ask for a rating,
use a [RadioGroup](/docs/components/radio-group/).
