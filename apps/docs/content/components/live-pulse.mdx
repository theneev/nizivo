## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

The children are the words ("Live", "Syncing"), and they are required: the pulse alone says
nothing. The ring repeats every 1.6 seconds; under Reduced motion only the dot shows.
