## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript: the slide is CSS. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

`items` are short labels or logos, drawn as pills with a dot (`bare` draws them as they are). Two
copies slide one set's width in 30 seconds and begin again unseen; the second copy is hidden from
assistive technology, so each item is read once. It pauses under the pointer and while anything in
it has focus, and stands still under Reduced motion.
