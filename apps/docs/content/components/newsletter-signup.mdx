## Static or interactive

**Client.** It is a form that waits for `onSubscribe`. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

`title` (h3 by default) and `description` say what people get; `note` what happens next. The field
is a [Field](/docs/components/field/) with an `type="email"` [Input](/docs/components/input/) and
`autocomplete="email"`, named by its visible label, never a placeholder alone. The browser's own
validation shows inline, in the words of `labels.missing` and `labels.invalid`. Subscribe is pending
while `onSubscribe` runs; if it throws, its message shows under the field, and on success a polite
live region says "Thanks — check your inbox to confirm."
