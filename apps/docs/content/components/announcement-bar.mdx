## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript: the close button calls `onDismiss`.
[How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

One message at a time, in `children`, with a link in `action` (drawn in text/inverse-accent and
underlined). It is a region named "Announcement". `dismissible` adds a close button named
"Dismiss announcement"; remembering the choice across visits is the app's.
