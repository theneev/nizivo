## Static or interactive

**Server.** No client JavaScript of its own. [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Use

`title` is the band's heading (h2 by default), `description` one line, and `actions` two
[Buttons](/docs/components/button/): one primary, one quiet. The design makes the primary one
Accent, since a Primary green sinks into the band. The band is dark in both themes, with
text/on-dark.

## The background

The design's background is the Aurora Dark shader: pass
`background={<ShaderSurface variant="aurora-dark" />}` ([ShaderSurface](/docs/components/shader-surface/))
and a soft dark scrim keeps the middle, where the text sits, dark. Without it the band is drawn with
CSS gradients; `background` takes any picture in its place, hidden from assistive technology.
