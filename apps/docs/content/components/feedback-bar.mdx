## Static or interactive

**Client.** The votes are [IconButtons](/docs/components/icon-button/) with `aria-pressed`, and the
reasons a [ChipGroup](/docs/components/chip-group/). [How to choose](/docs/guides/static-or-interactive/).

## Votes

`value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` hold the vote: `'up'`, `'down'` or `null`. Pressing the
pressed vote clears it. The buttons keep their names ("Good answer", "Bad answer"); the prompt
changes to a thanks, and a screen reader hears it. A vote never opens a dialog or moves focus.

## Reasons

Pass `reasons` to offer them after a thumbs down, one to pick; `reason`, `defaultReason` and
`onReasonChange` hold it. Keep them few and short: "Not accurate", "Too long".
