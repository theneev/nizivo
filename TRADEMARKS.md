## What you may do without asking

- Say that your product uses, works with or is built on Nizivo, as long as you do not suggest that Neev Technologies endorses or sponsors it.
- Redistribute unmodified, official Nizivo packages under their original names.
- Refer to Nizivo by name in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews.

## What needs a new name

If you fork Nizivo and publish or distribute a modified version, you must rename it. That means:

- a product name that is not "Nizivo" and is not confusingly similar to it;
- package names outside the `@nizivo` scope;
- no Nizivo logo, and nothing that looks like an official Nizivo release.

You may say your fork is "based on Nizivo".

## Questions

For anything not covered here, open an issue at [github.com/theneev/nizivo](https://github.com/theneev/nizivo/issues) before you use the name or logo.
