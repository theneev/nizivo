## Surface glass: two effect styles, two uses

- **`Nizivo/Surface/Glass`**: a 24 px background blur, then a drop shadow `0 16px 40px -8px` in
  rgb(16 19 17) at 10 %. Used on the Lab's **Glass card** 7275:19634 (09 Lab): fill white at 42 %, a
  1 px stroke white at 70 %, radius 28. The Lab's note: "Always on a tinted card, never on white."
- **Site Navbar `Style=Floating`** 7392:80 (13 Marketing): "glass pill (bg/surface 72% + background
  blur) that sits over shader heroes", effects background blur 24 and a drop shadow 24.
- **`Nizivo/Surface/Liquid Glass`**: a Figma GLASS effect (refraction 0.25, depth 14, dispersion
  0.12). It has no CSS equivalent and no consumer; it stays in `@nizivo/lab` (§5).
- **`Nizivo/Surface/Inner Highlight`**: an inner shadow `0 1px 0` white at 70 %, unused.
- No blur or backdrop variable exists (§7: the normalizer skips a `blur` effect).

## Funnel Chart 751:319 (03 Data Visualization)

- 748 x 180, four steps 12 px apart. Each step: the label (Label/M, text/secondary, 150 px), a track
  (chart/track, 420 x 36, radius 8) holding the bar (chart/primary), the count (Numeric/M,

## Heatmap 751:393 (03 Data Visualization)

- 500 x 322: seven day rows (Label/M, text/tertiary, 44 px) of twelve 32 px cells (radius 6, 6 px
  apart), an hours row (Mono/M, text/tertiary, every other label empty) and a "Fewer … More" scale.
- **The ramp is chart/primary at five opacities: 0.08, 0.25, 0.45, 0.70, 1.00** (cells and the five
  scale swatches). So the "sequential chart/seq-1..5" gap (§14) is a ramp of alphas on one token,
  not five solid colours; solid steps for dark stay a builder question (M65).
- Description: "Sequential ramp chart/* light→dark with a visible legend. Each cell is focusable (or
  a table alternative is provided) and announces day, hour and value. Never the only view of the data."

## Date Range Picker 746:267 (02 Actions & Forms): the popover only

- 525 x 386, padding 20, gap 16, radius/xl, bg/surface-raised, border/subtle, Elevation/3 Popover.
  The trigger is Select's (docs/figma/02, "What Select really is").
- **Presets** (167 wide, 16 px right padding and a border/subtle line): Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days,
  Last 30 days, This quarter, Year to date; rows 36 px, 8 / 10 px padding, radius/sm, Body/M; the
  chosen one bg/brand-subtle with text/brand.
- **Calendar** 296 wide: a month row (chevron-left, "October 2026" in Label/M Strong, chevron-right),
  weekday initials "Mo … Su" (Label/M, text/tertiary, 42 x 28) and six weeks of 42 x 36 cells.
- **Day cells:** default Body/M text/primary; outside the month text/disabled; range start and end
  bg/brand, radius/sm, Label/M Strong text/on-brand; in range bg/brand-subtle, square; today
  text/brand with a 4 px bg/brand dot below. **Not drawn:** unavailable, hover, focus.
- **Footer:** the range as text ("Oct 1 – Oct 7, 2026", Body/M Strong), Cancel (Button Secondary Md)
  and Apply (Button Primary Md). One month is shown.
- Answers two of §16.3's open questions (Apply / Cancel, one month); compare-to-previous-period and
  the unavailable state are still not drawn, so the picker stays `unstable/` as planned.
- Description: "Calendar is a grid: arrows move by day, Page Up/Down by month, Home/End to week
  edges. Selected range uses bg/brand + text/on-brand; today is outlined, not colour-only. Announce
  the chosen range in the trigger." (Today is drawn with a dot, not an outline.)
