# Asset provenance ledger

No image, render, photo, illustration or other binary asset from the Figma file ships anywhere (packages, docs, Storybook, the registry, fixtures or a Figma Community copy) until it has an entry here (ARCHITECTURE.md §6, §14.3, §19.3). CI fails on any binary under `apps/**/public` or `registry/**` without an entry.

The 3D renders, product photos and portraits in the Figma file have unknown provenance and stay out until each one is cleared.
