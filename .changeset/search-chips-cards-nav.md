SearchField (`./search-field`): a `<search>` landmark around a native `<input type="search">`, 32, 40 or 48 px, with a magnifier, a clear button (Escape clears too, in every engine) and an optional `shortcut` drawn with Kbd and announced through `aria-keyshortcuts`; `pending` shows a spinner. In a `<form method="get">` it filters with no JavaScript; inside a Field it is labelled, described and validated by the Field. ChipGroup and Chip (`./chip-group`): pill choices as native radios, or checkboxes with `multiple`, with a `leading` swatch slot and an `unavailable` chip that keeps its colour, is struck through and can still be chosen; a group control (Field `group`). ChoiceCardGroup and ChoiceCard (`./choice-card`): cards around real visible radios, with `leading`, `description` and `trailing` slots (a "Default" Badge is composition); a Field's error marks the chosen card invalid. Selection never changes a chip's or card's size. NavItem (`./nav-item`): a server-safe sidebar link, `aria-current="page"` when `current`, a router link through `render`, an `icon`, a `count` and `disabled`, at a fixed 40 px; an ancestor's `data-collapsed` (the future Sidebar's) folds it to the icon rail.
